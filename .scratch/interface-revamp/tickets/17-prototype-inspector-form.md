## Resolution

On 2026-10-16 the human accepted the responsive B inspector snapshot `be8bef3e9efaea76b986480a40de6bd260e59d18`. Its focused production-browser run passed 11/11 cases. The accepted rendering has visible sections, full-width Name/Description and multiline custom text, distinct catalogue/custom values, persistent bottom actions and error access, top Hide and separate reviewed deletion.

Acceptance did not establish OS-keyboard, physical-device, full feature-parity or product-wide behavior. Those remain separate validation obligations.
