## Resolution

The focused controls prototype supplied nine browser cases for repeated Add, Navigate versus Select, right-button/two-contact view navigation with route-origin retention, Escape layering and compact exclusive panels. Separate Dock checks covered target placement, anchored chooser and dismissal. The human-selected Dock and current review supported specification of the resolved tools contract.

Physical second-contact behavior, full accessibility, full product parity, persistence/recovery and complete exports were not established. These bounded prototype checks are design evidence, not product acceptance or a current-head test total.
