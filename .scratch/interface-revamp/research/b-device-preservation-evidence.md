### Targeted gesture arbitration review

The parent explicitly retained **one-finger touch editing in Move** while the corrective agent works. Replacing it with touch navigation and mouse-only editing would violate the accepted interaction contract. SOL 6.1 High supplies analysis only; Luna XHIGH owns implementation. One focused diff review is planned, without a new broad measurement campaign.

Inspection of the installed laboratory D3 sources (`d3-drag/src/drag.js`, `d3-zoom/src/zoom.js`, `d3-drag/src/noevent.js`) establishes the relevant behavior: drag filtering occurs at gesture start; subsequent touchmove/end of an already-started gesture do not rerun the filter; `touchcancel` reaches the same end dispatch as release; touch handlers call `stopImmediatePropagation()`. Merely restricting a start filter to one contact neither cancels an active edit nor ensures ancestor zoom receives the native sequence. These are implementation-source facts, not physical-input results.

The corrective review checks explicit edit/view ownership through second-contact capture: retain actual one-finger editing, restore uncommitted preview to applied geometry when the second contact appears, suppress release publication and clicks for that canceled sequence, and let only pan/pinch alter the view until full release. Do not undo earlier applied work, reset the active tool, or close the draft. D3 cleanup must not replay canceled geometry. Long press must request Inspect directly, cancel on motion/scroll/second contact/interruption, and suppress the following synthetic click rather than using it to activate inspection. Navigation context opening remains separate from panel opening. Mobile full-height panels retain draft/context/selection and hide the rail; desktop map navigation remains available alongside editing.
